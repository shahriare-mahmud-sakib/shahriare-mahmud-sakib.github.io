## Project overview

This capstone project proposed a **community-level municipal solid-waste management system for Lalbag, Dhaka**. The work combined sustainable waste-management planning with structural engineering so the proposed processing and recycling facilities could be assessed functionally and structurally.

## Project objectives

- Develop a practical and sustainable community-level waste-management concept.
- Study the feasibility of the proposed system for Lalbag, Dhaka.
- Plan facilities for waste processing, separation, recovery, and recycling.
- Analyse the structural response of the proposed facilities.
- Design suitable RCC and steel-truss structural components.

## Scope of work

The project included municipal waste-management planning, feasibility assessment, structural modelling, analysis, and design. Revit and SAP2000 were used in the modelling and structural-analysis workflow.

## My role

As **group leader**, I coordinated the team’s work and contributed to the planning, feasibility study, structural analysis, and structural-design tasks.

## Structural engineering component

- RCC structural systems for the proposed waste-processing facilities.
- Steel-truss systems appropriate for the required facility spaces.
- Structural models developed and evaluated using Revit and SAP2000.
