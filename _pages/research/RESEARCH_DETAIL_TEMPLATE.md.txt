HOW TO ADD A NEW COMPLETED OR CURRENT RESEARCH ITEM
===================================================

1. Add the complete item to `_data/research_page.yml` under either:
   - `completed_research: items:` or
   - `current_research: items:`
2. Give the newest item `order: 1` and increase older order numbers.
3. Copy this file and place the copy under `_pages/research/completed/` or
   `_pages/research/current/`.
4. Rename it with a short lowercase slug and change `.txt` to `.md`.
5. Delete these instructions and edit the front matter below.

---
layout: research-detail
title: "Exact Research Title"
permalink: /research/current/your-research-slug/
author_profile: true
research_type: "current"
research_slug: "your-research-slug"
---

The `research_slug` must exactly match the `slug` in `_data/research_page.yml`.
Use `research_type: "completed"` for a completed-research detail page.
