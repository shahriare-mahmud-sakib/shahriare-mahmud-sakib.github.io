Place optional research resources inside this folder.

Suggested structure:

files/research/
├── undergraduate-research/
│   ├── presentation.pdf
│   ├── poster.pdf
│   └── summary.pdf
├── current-research/
│   ├── project-summary.pdf
│   └── presentation.pdf
└── future-research/
    └── research-vision.pdf

After adding a file, create a resource entry in `_data/research_page.yml`.

Example:

resources:
  - label: "View Research Presentation"
    url: "/files/research/undergraduate-research/presentation.pdf"
    icon: "fas fa-display"
    external: false
