Place optional research files in this folder, for example:

files/research/completed/
  presentation.pdf
  poster.pdf
  research-summary.pdf

files/research/current/
  project-summary.pdf
  presentation.pdf

Then add a resource inside the relevant item in `_data/research_page.yml`:

resources:
  - label: "View Research Presentation"
    url: "/files/research/completed/presentation.pdf"
    icon: "fas fa-display"
    external: false
